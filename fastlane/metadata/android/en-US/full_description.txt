Just Einbürgerungstest helps you prepare for the German citizenship test (Einbürgerungstest) and the “Leben in Deutschland” test – free, without ads, without tracking and fully offline.

<b>All questions on your device</b>
All 300 general questions plus the 10 questions for your federal state from the official catalog. You choose your state on first launch and can change it at any time.

<b>Daily 10</b>
Ten questions a day in about three minutes, mixing due reviews with new questions. Questions you got wrong come back at the end of the round.

<b>Learning with boxes</b>
A three-box Leitner system repeats mistakes right away and known questions less often. The home screen shows how many questions are in each box, and you can practise any box directly.

<b>Catalog</b>
Practise by topic (politics, history, society, your federal state), your mistakes or your bookmarks.

<b>Exam simulation</b>
33 questions in 60 minutes, like the real test: 30 general and 3 state questions. You can change answers and flag questions until you submit. Afterwards you see your score and every mistake; past results are kept.

<b>Progress</b>
Your daily streak, answers over the last seven days and your next goal at a glance.

<b>Translations</b>
Questions and answers can be shown in English or Ukrainian. The app itself is in German, the language of the test, and the exam simulation is German only.

<b>Privacy</b>
The app has no internet permission. Your progress stays on your device.
